- ## What it is
	- **The Root** is a metaphysical location that exists outside of time and human concepts. It is the source of all events and phenomena in the universe
		- Known as the Swirl of the Root, The Root, the Akashic Records, or simply Akasha
		- Also referred to as the "Outside of the World"
	- It is a collection of all the experiences and desires of everyone who has ever (or will have ever) lived, and could aptly be referred to as humanity's collective subconscious
		- Information of all events, possibilities, thoughts, words, emotions, and intent to have ever occurred in the past, present, and future of the world are archived and stored in the Root of Akasha
		- It's important to note that the Akashic Records contains the desires and experiences of all entities and life forms – not just humans
	- It is the place where all souls come from, and where they return after death
		- This includes the souls of [Heroic Spirits]([[Servants]]) inscribed on the [[Throne of Heroes]]
	- Most Mages have the ultimate ambition of reaching the Root, and many of them focus their experiments on discovering a path to it
- ## Reaching the Root
	- There are two main goals for Mages who strive to reach the Root:
		- To acquire the capacity to perform [[True Magic]]
		  logseq.order-list-type:: number
		- To touch the Root itself and understand the "truth" of the universe inside of it
		  logseq.order-list-type:: number
	- Various methods have been used by Mages in their attempts to reach the Root. It is usually done by either reaching the pinnacle of a certain concept, or by manipulating certain magical systems
		- One example of this would be the [[Holy Grail War]], which uses the [[Holy Grail]] to harness the power generated by defeated Heroic Spirits to make a hole to the Root
		- Each route functions like a single-access token[^1], in that once a method has been established and used, it is closed for good and can never be used again
		- If the route isn't entered, it can be left open until someone else claims it, after which it closes off for good
	- ### True Magic
		- Magic (also known as the ability to perform miraculous feats that would be otherwise impossible for science or human ability to achieve) is attained by reaching the Swirl of the Root, but not touching it
			- Moreover, in order to acquire Magic, the Mage must be the very first to travel through a specific path to the Root;
			- even if the same theory and method are used to reach the Root, those who came after the first Mage using a certain path cannot acquire Magic.
		- The First Magic and Fifth Magic were the result of reaching the Root, while the Second Magic, Third Magic, and Fourth Magic were meant as a means to reach it.
		- Magic was a goal for many Mages before the creation of the Fifth Magic
			- First Magic: who knows. you die so hard you loop right back around to being unkillable
			- Second Magic: parallel worlds
			- Third Magic: materialization of the soul / immortality
			- Fourth Magic: who knows. black hole bullshit im calling it now
			- Fifth Magic: time travel of a sort. running a scam on time
		- Once achieved, Magic can be inherited through family bloodline
	- ### Touching the Root
		- Seeing the Root, touching it and then managing to understand it will completely erase the meaning of "impossible", granting the magus what some would describe as "the capabilities of God".
			- Needless to say, this is impossible for even the most talented Magi.
		- Those who have touched it directly have never returned to the world.
			- Those who have managed to touch it ceased to exist on the spot, as their human souls are either going back to "where they came from", becoming absorbed into the Root, or something similar.
			- The exact details are unknown, so even Magi who have left their names in history have refrained from touching it.
		- There are also Magicians who have come close to it, but backed away before touching it, and Magi who have gotten close to it to stabilize their own Magecraft in a hurry.
			- Aoko believes that it is possible those who end up there "become God" in a sense, so there is no reason for them to come back.
			- It is also possible they cannot come back because "being God" isn't truly such a wondrous event as humans may think.
			- Both those who succeeded and failed touched it and couldn't return to the world.
			- It may be they told people about it is because the first person to become God wanted companions out of loneliness, or that their work on that side was so massive that they needed other companions.
